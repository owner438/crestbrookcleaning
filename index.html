      Terms of Service

      Welcome to Crestbrook House Cleaning Services. By booking or using our services, you agree to the terms below. These terms are in place to ensure clarity, fairness, and professionalism in every interaction.

      1. Booking & Payment

      All services must be booked through our official platform. Full payment is required upon confirmation unless pre-approved. Additional fees may apply for extra services or unusual conditions (e.g., excessive clutter, pet-related messes).

      2. Cancellations & Rescheduling

      We require at least 24 hours' notice for cancellations or rescheduling. Late cancellations (within 24 hours) may result in a fee of up to 50% of the scheduled service. Repeated short-notice cancellations may affect future booking eligibility.

      3. Entry to Property

      You must ensure we have secure, safe, and legal access to your property at the scheduled time. Failure to do so may result in a missed appointment fee. If key codes or special entry instructions are required, they must be shared in advance.

      4. Service Limitations

      We do not provide outdoor cleaning, biohazard removal, or heavy lifting. Services are limited to agreed tasks per booking. Any changes should be communicated in advance. We reserve the right to refuse service in unsafe or unsanitary conditions.

      5. Satisfaction Guarantee

      If you're not completely satisfied with the cleaning, please contact us within 24 hours. We will revisit the location and address the issue free of charge. After 24 hours, corrections may incur additional fees at our discretion.

      6. Liability & Damages

      Our staff is insured and trained to treat your property with care. However, we are not liable for damage caused by normal wear, unstable fixtures, or items not disclosed as fragile. Please secure valuables and inform us of any special handling needs.

      6a. Reporting Damage or Loss

      If you believe an item was damaged or lost during a cleaning, please notify us in writing at within 48 hours of the service, including a description and photographs of the affected item(s). Prompt notice allows us to investigate accurately, and we may ask to inspect the item before it is repaired, replaced, or discarded. Verified claims are handled by Crestbrook and, where applicable, through our insurance. Claims reported more than 48 hours after service, or after the item has already been repaired, replaced, or discarded before we can inspect it, may not be eligible.

      7. Pets

      We love pets! However, for safety, please inform us in advance if pets will be on the premises. Aggressive animals must be secured during service.

      8. Privacy

      Your personal information is handled with care. We never sell your data. For full details, see our Privacy Policy.

      8a. Communications & Text Messaging

      By providing your phone number through any of our forms, you agree that Crestbrook House Cleaning Services may send you transactional text messages (booking confirmations, reminders, schedule changes, payment receipts) tied to the service you've requested. With your separate, affirmative opt-in, we may also send promotional texts. You can opt out of marketing texts at any time by replying STOP or adjusting your preferences in your customer portal. Reply HELP for support. Message frequency varies; message and data rates may apply. For full disclosure see Section 3a of our Privacy Policy.

      9. Right to Refuse Service

      We reserve the right to refuse or discontinue services for any reason, including but not limited to unsafe conditions, harassment, or failure to comply with our terms.

      9a. Non-Solicitation of Our Cleaners

      The cleaners we assign to your home are recruited, background-screened, trained, and supported by Crestbrook at significant cost. While you receive services from us, and for 24 months after your most recent cleaning, you agree not to directly or indirectly solicit, hire, or engage any cleaner introduced to you through Crestbrook to perform cleaning services outside of our platform. If a cleaner is hired or engaged in violation of this section, you agree to pay Crestbrook a placement and training fee of $2,500 per cleaner. This fee is a reasonable estimate of our recruiting, screening, and training investment and is not intended as a penalty. This provision restricts you as our customer and does not limit any cleaner's own right to work.

      Privacy Policy

      At Crestbrook House Cleaning Services ("Crestbrook", "we", "us"), your privacy, trust, and clarity matter deeply. This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect, how we use it, when we share it, how we protect it, and what rights you have. It applies across our website, booking forms, communications (email, phone), and any associated services under Crestbrook.

      1. Personal Information We Collect

      Identifiers & Contact Details: your name, email, phone, service address, and login credentials if you use an account.

      Service & Transaction Details: requested service types, scheduling preferences, special instructions, service notes, invoices, and tokenized payment references (not full card numbers).

      Communications: messages via forms, email, or phone, including support and follow-up notes.

      Technical & Usage Data: IP address, browser type, device model, visits to website pages, interactions, and analytics from cookies or similar tools.

      Referral & Marketing Context: how you reached our site (ads, links, campaigns).

      Optional Photos: any "before/after" images you share to help with estimates, quality assurance, or damage documentation.

      2. How & When We Collect Your Data

      When you request a quote, submit a booking inquiry, complete forms, or sign in. When you communicate with us via phone, email, chat, or social platforms. Automatically while you browse our website through cookies and analytics. From integrated service partners like payment processors or scheduling tools when needed to deliver services.

      3. Why We Use Your Information

      Service Delivery: scheduling cleanings, matching cleaners, processing payments, and providing support.

      Communication: sending confirmations, reminders, and responding to inquiries via email or phone.

      Improvement & Security: analyzing site performance, troubleshooting issues, preventing fraud, and refining service offerings.

      Promotions (optional): sending marketing messages where you've given permission (you can opt out anytime).

      Legal & Compliance: retaining records to meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, or enforce our policies.

      3a. SMS / Text Messaging (TCPA Disclosure)

      When you provide your phone number through our booking forms, you may receive service-related text messages from Crestbrook House Cleaning Services — for example booking confirmations, reminders, schedule changes, and payment receipts. These transactional messages are sent in connection with the cleaning service you've requested and are permitted under the established business relationship exception of the federal Telephone Consumer Protection Act (TCPA).

      If you affirmatively consent (by checking the SMS opt-in box at sign-up or telling our team you'd like texts), we may also send promotional messages such as occasional offers, follow-ups, or service updates. You can withdraw consent for marketing texts at any time by replying STOP to any of our messages or by adjusting your communication preferences in your customer portal. Reply HELP for assistance. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply, charged by your wireless carrier.

      Crestbrook House Cleaning Services does not sell or share your phone number with third parties for marketing purposes.

      4. Legal Basis for Processing

      We process data based on your consent, contract needs (booking services), legitimate interests (e.g., improvements, fraud detection), or to comply with legal obligations.

      5. Who We Share Data With

      We do not sell your personal information for money, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing. We do use advertising and analytics tools that transmit certain data to their providers to measure and improve our own marketing and recruiting — some privacy laws classify this kind of transfer as a "sale" or "share."       • Payment & scheduling partners (such as Stripe) to process bookings and payments.
      • Communications providers (email and text-messaging platforms) to send notifications.
      • The cleaning professionals assigned to your service, for the job details needed to perform it.
      • Advertising & analytics providers — Meta (Facebook) and Google — as described in Section 8.
      • Service providers (IT, security, hosting) under confidentiality agreements.
      • Legal or law enforcement entities when required by law.

Customer account data is retained for the duration of the customer relationship plus up to 3 years. You may request deletion of your data at any time (see Section 13).

      7. Data Security

      We implement industry-standard technical and organizational safeguards to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, and destruction. These include encrypted data transmission (HTTPS), access controls, and secure payment processing via PCI-compliant processors. No method of internet transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

      Our website uses cookies and similar technologies to keep the site working, understand how it is used, and measure and improve our advertising and recruiting. These fall into three groups:

      • Essential cookies: required for the site to function (e.g., session management).
      • Analytics cookies: help us understand how visitors use the site.
      • Advertising cookies & technologies: help us measure and target our own marketing and recruiting.

      Specifically, we use these third-party tools, which receive data as described:

      • Meta (Facebook) Pixel: a script that runs on our pages and reports your visit and activity on our site — together with your IP address, device and browser information, and Meta advertising cookies — to Meta, so we can measure our ads and build advertising audiences.
      • Meta Conversions API (server-side): if you apply to work with us through our careers pages, we also send certain application events and hashed (irreversibly scrambled) contact details — such as your email, phone, name, and general location — to Meta directly from our server, to measure and improve our recruiting ads. Because this happens on our server rather than in your browser, ad blockers and browser settings do not stop it.
      • Google Analytics: reports site traffic and usage to Google so we can improve the site.

      You can limit browser-based tracking through your browser's cookie settings and through ad-blocking or opt-out tools; Meta and Google also offer ad-preference controls within your account settings on those platforms.
